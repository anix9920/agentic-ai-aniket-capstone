# Approval Policy

CloudOptima AI Approval Policy

1. Approval Levels
   - Level 1 (Automated): Shutdown of resources with < 5% CPU and confirmed idle
   - Level 2 (Team Lead): Downsize/rightsizing recommendations with 20-50% savings
   - Level 3 (Manager): Scale-up recommendations and storage policy changes
   - Level 4 (Director): Architecture changes, reserved instance purchases

2. Approval Criteria
   - Must include business impact analysis
   - Must show estimated savings with confidence level
   - Must have rollback plan documented
   - Must not violate SLO commitments

3. Auto-Approval Rules
   - Resources tagged with "auto-optimize=true"
   - Development environments during off-hours
   - Savings < $50/month with < 5% performance impact

4. Rejection Reasons
   - Insufficient business justification
   - High risk to availability SLOs
   - Missing rollback procedure
   - Conflicting active incidents

5. Audit Trail
   - All approvals logged with timestamp, approver, rationale
   - Quarterly audit of approval decisions
   - Metrics: approval rate, implementation success, actual savings
